Problem Statement

Campus Safety & Harassment Reporting System

Build a secure, confidential digital platform that helps students report safety concerns — and automatically escalates repeated incidents to the right authority.

🛡️ Challenge Brief

Students may sometimes experience repeated unwanted following, harassment, or uncomfortable behaviour on or around campus. In many cases, they hesitate to report it because they are unsure whom to contact or are worried about their personal information being widely shared.

We need a secure and simple digital reporting system that helps students confidentially report safety concerns and ensures the issue is escalated to the right authority when repeated reports are received.

📋 Two Types of Complaints

🏫 Offline Ragging

Physical / In-Person Harassment
  • Following / Stalking
  • Unwanted behaviour
  • Threats / Intimidation
  • Other offline incidents

💻 Online Ragging

Digital / Cyber Harassment
  • Fake accounts
  • Obscene content
  • Online threats
  • Other cyber incidents

⚠️ Online ragging complaints must include screenshot proof (if available).

⚙️ Escalation Workflow

Based on repeated reports, the system automatically notifies the right authority.

1st Report

→ HOD

Student submits complaint. HOD receives the report (without victim details).

2nd Report

→ Dean

Dean receives the report (without victim details).

3rd Report

→ Higher Authority

Higher authority receives the report with full details (if required).

  • ✅ Builds a safer campus for everyone
  • ✅ Encourages students to speak up without fear
  • ✅ Helps authorities act faster and more effectively
